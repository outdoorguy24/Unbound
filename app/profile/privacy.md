# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Unbound app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was developed by Unbound App LLC (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS".

## What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

### User Provided Information
The Application obtains the information you provide when you download, register, and use the Application. Registration is optional but required for certain features. This information may include:
- Email address for account creation
- User preferences for content blocking
- Custom website URLs you choose to block
- Schedule settings for blocking periods
- Device settings and configurations

The Service Provider may use this information to:
- Provide and maintain the service
- Improve screen time management features
- Send important notifications about your blocking schedules
- Communicate essential service updates

### Automatically Collected Information
The Application collects certain information automatically, including:
- Device information (type, model, operating system)
- App usage statistics and screen time data
- Blocking effectiveness metrics
- VPN connection data (when content blocking is active)
- App installation and usage patterns
- Basic crash reporting data

## Screen Time and App Usage Data

The Application collects screen time and app usage data to:
- Generate usage reports and statistics
- Provide insights into your digital wellness
- Improve blocking effectiveness
- Customize recommendations for better time management

This data is:
- Stored securely
- Used only for providing and improving the service
- Never sold to third parties
- Anonymized when used for analysis

## Content Blocking and VPN Usage

When you enable content blocking features:
- The Application monitors network traffic for blocked content
- VPN configurations are used solely for content filtering
- No browsing data is stored or logged
- Only blocking events are recorded (time, success/failure)

## Third-Party Services

The Application uses these third-party services:
- Google Analytics for Firebase
- Firebase Crashlytics
- App Store Payment Processing
- Supabase for database management
- VPN Services (when applicable)

Each service processes limited data according to their respective privacy policies and our data protection requirements.

## Data Retention and Deletion

The Service Provider:
- Retains user data while your account is active
- Stores screen time data for up to 12 months
- Keeps blocking preferences until you change them
- Allows you to request data deletion at any time
